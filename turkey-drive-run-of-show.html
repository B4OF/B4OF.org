Donation Flow

  1. Donors sign up at b4of.org (Tokens for Turkeys page).
  2. Once signups come in, post the official Bitcoin address and QR code on the page.
  3. Donations go to a wallet B4OF controls (ideally more than one person with access). Do a small test deposit first.
  4. Convert the Bitcoin to dollars, buy turkeys, and announce the date and address to signups.
