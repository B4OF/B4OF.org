About

We build people. Then they build the future.

Building 4 Our Future Foundation is a Chicago nonprofit focused on financial literacy, entrepreneur development and youth mentorship in under-resourced communities.

Our mission

To give people in under-resourced communities the knowledge, the network and the confidence to build lasting financial security and own their future.

We believe wealth starts with understanding. When a young person learns how money works, or a first-time founder gets real guidance, the benefits reach whole families and neighborhoods.

Learn

Practical education in plain language, with no jargon and no gatekeeping.

Build

Hands-on support for savings, businesses and community projects.

Lead

Mentors and role models who help young people see what is possible.

What we stand for

The values behind every class, session and conversation.

Access

Great programs should be reachable for every family, whatever their income.

Ownership

We teach people to build assets and businesses they control.

Mentorship

Real relationships change outcomes. Young people need adults who show up.

Integrity

We keep our programs honest, safe and focused on the people we serve.

Our education partner

B4OF works alongside Block Wall Street, a Chicago-based crypto education and community organization, to bring modern money education, including digital assets, to the people who need it most.

  • Free and low-cost community classes
