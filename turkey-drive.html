Tokens for Turkeys

A Bitcoin turkey drive. Donate Bitcoin and we turn it into turkeys and put them on Chicago tables before Thanksgiving. Then join us for breakfast and a free intro class on Bitcoin and blockchain.

Date & location announced after donations are received

How Tokens for Turkeys Works

  1. You donate Bitcoin to the Building 4 Our Future Foundation donation address below.
  2. We convert the donations into turkeys and purchase them for families in Chicago.
  3. We hold a community event about a week before Thanksgiving, with breakfast, a one-hour Bitcoin class, and turkey giveaway.
  4. Families pick up their turkeys at the event.

The Event

Breakfast

A free community breakfast with pancakes, eggs, and more. Final menu coming soon.

Intro to Bitcoin Class

A one-hour beginner-friendly class on Bitcoin and blockchain, taught by Block Wall Street with curriculum from Sun School of Satoshi. Everyone takes home a one-page Bitcoin basics sheet.

Turkey Giveaway

Turkeys purchased with donated Bitcoin are given out to families at the end of the event.
