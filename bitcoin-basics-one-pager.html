What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is digital money that no bank or government controls. Anyone can send it to anyone, anywhere, any time of day. There will only ever be 21 million bitcoin, which is why many people see it as digital gold.

What is a blockchain?

A blockchain is a public record book of every Bitcoin transaction. Thousands of computers around the world keep identical copies, so no one can cheat or erase an entry.

Key words

  • Wallet: an app or device that holds your keys and lets you send and receive Bitcoin.
  • Address: like an account number you share so people can send you Bitcoin. Safe to share.
  • Private key / recovery phrase: your password to your money. Never share it with anyone.
  • Satoshi (sat): the smallest piece of Bitcoin. You can own a small piece without buying a whole coin.

Stay safe

  • Nobody legitimate will ever ask for your recovery phrase. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • Write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it somewhere safe. Never take a photo of it or save it online.
  • Buy hardware wallets only from the maker or an authorized store.
  • Send a small test amount first.
  • If someone promises guaranteed returns, walk away.

How to get started

  • Download a trusted wallet app and write down your recovery phrase.
  • Start small and learn before you invest more.
